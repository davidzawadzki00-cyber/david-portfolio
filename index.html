02 / Introduction

A little about me.

Brooklyn, New York
Hunter College
Emerging Media

I’m David, an Emerging Media student at Hunter College, based in Brooklyn, New York. I’m interested in visual storytelling, media, communications, culture, and creative strategy—and how these fields shape the way we see and connect with one another.

My approach starts with observation: paying attention to everyday details, asking questions, and experimenting with different ways to communicate an idea. I’m drawn to work that considers the audience’s experience as carefully as its visual form. As I develop my practice, I’m exploring how thoughtful images, digital content, and interactive experiences can make a story feel clear, engaging, and personal.

03 / Perspective

Artist statement

I work across photography, video, animation, creative coding, and web design to explore how people express identity and build a sense of belonging. Living in Brooklyn, I am drawn to everyday details: the visual character of a neighborhood, the routines people share, and the ways a group can make a public space feel personal.

My process begins with observing and asking questions. I choose a medium based on what I want the viewer to notice or experience. In photography and video, I think about framing, pacing, and the relationship between sound and image. In animation and code, I experiment with movement, repetition, and interaction. Working across these forms lets me approach a subject from different directions and learn through revision.

As an Emerging Media student at Hunter College, I am developing a practice that connects visual experimentation with clear communication. My interests in journalism and creative strategy shape how I think about audiences and the context around an image. I want my work to encourage people to look more closely at familiar places and experiences, and to consider how media influences what we notice.
